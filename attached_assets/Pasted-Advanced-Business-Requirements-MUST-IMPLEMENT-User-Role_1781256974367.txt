Kenya Location Hierarchy (VERY IMPORTANT)

The location system must be hierarchical.

Do NOT show all locations at once.

Implement dependent dropdowns.

County Dropdown

When a user selects a County:

Only that county's Constituencies should become available.

Example:

County:

Nairobi

Then Constituencies:

Westlands
Dagoretti North
Dagoretti South
Lang’ata
Embakasi North
Embakasi South
Embakasi East
Embakasi West
Embakasi Central
Kasarani
Makadara
Kamukunji
Starehe
Ruaraka
Roysambu
Kibra
Mathare

No constituencies from other counties should appear.

Constituency Dropdown

After a constituency is selected:

Only related wards/locations should appear.

Example:

County:

Nairobi

Constituency:

Westlands

Then show only:

Parklands/Highridge
Karura
Kangemi
Mountain View
